**OLLYCONN LLC** is the independent engineering practice of John Connolly, based in New York.

I step in as technical lead when a software delivery is under pressure: the architecture needs a decision, the integrations need to hold, and the team needs someone who will also write the code.

## What I do

- **Hands-on technical leadership.** Architecture decisions, design reviews, and the pull requests that go with them.
- **Integration engineering.** APIs, data flows and cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure) that connect new software to the systems a business already runs on.
- **Code review.** Independent review of codebases and pull requests, with clear, prioritized findings.
- **Handover that sticks.** Documentation, runbooks and pairing, so the team owns what gets built.

## How I work

Scoped in writing, with set core hours and one point of contact. Remote, on Eastern time, billed hourly.
