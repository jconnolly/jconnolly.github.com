*Last updated November 2, 2026.*

**Program name:** Rebbe alerts, from OLLYCONN LLC.

**What you'll receive:** account and schedule notifications for OLLYCONN's own work-hours tracking, such as "You're one hour from your weekly limit" or "An approval request is ready to review." No marketing.

**Who can receive them:** OLLYCONN's owner only, after opting in from the Rebbe settings page.

**How often:** varies with activity; typically a few messages per working day, and no more than about 30 per day.

**Cost:** message and data rates may apply. OLLYCONN does not charge for these messages.

**Opt out:** reply **STOP** to any message to stop receiving them. You'll get one confirmation message and nothing after that.

**Help:** reply **HELP**, or email [support@ollyconn.com](mailto:support@ollyconn.com).

**Carriers** are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

**Privacy:** see the [SMS privacy policy](/sms/privacy/).
