*Last updated November 2, 2026.*

OLLYCONN LLC ("OLLYCONN", "we") sends text messages from its toll-free number for **Rebbe**, an internal notification service. This page explains what we collect for those messages and what we do with it.

## who gets these messages

Rebbe sends work-hours and schedule alerts to OLLYCONN's owner only. It is not offered to the public, and we do not send marketing messages.

## what we collect

- The mobile phone number that opted in to receive alerts.
- The content of the alerts we send, and delivery status from the carrier.

## how we use it

Only to deliver the alerts that number asked for, and to troubleshoot delivery.

## sharing

**We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or any other purpose.** Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with anyone. We use Amazon Web Services to send the messages; AWS processes the number only to deliver them.

## keeping and deleting

We keep the number while alerts are active. Reply **STOP** at any time to opt out; we then stop sending and delete the number from the alert list within 30 days.

## help and contact

Reply **HELP** for help, or email [privacy@ollyconn.com](mailto:privacy@ollyconn.com). See also the [SMS terms](/sms/terms/).
